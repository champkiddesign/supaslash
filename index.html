        Welcome

        Welcome to SupaSlash

        Welcome to SupaSlash, where you can park the chaos, line up what actually matters, and slash through it one task at a time.

        It’s a floating timer that hangs out on your desktop until you say go. Then it’s just you, the clock, and the next thing. Client work, a side hustle, or the garage you’ve been threatening to organize — name it, time it, and get paid for it if you want. Future-you already likes this plan.

        Sprint is focus with a finish line. Every task gets a slice of time, lined up in a bar along the edge of the screen. You race that slice, then roll straight into the next one.

        Leave it on if you like a little friendly pressure. You can flip it later in Labs.

        Sprint

        Just you — the human doing the slashing. Add your name so SupaSlash knows who to cheer for. Getting paid? Turn Billable on and drop in your hourly rate. We’ll count the hours while you count the wins.

        Business name, email, address, and logo can wait in Personal Profile. Skip this if you just want to get moving.
